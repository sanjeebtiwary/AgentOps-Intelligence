# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in AgentOps Intelligence to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus technical discussions on ideas, evidence, and implementation.
- Accept reasonable criticism and differing viewpoints.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Help keep discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or degrading language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting technical discussions.
- Repeatedly engaging in abusive or hostile conduct.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an appropriate contact channel. Avoid posting sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers should review reports fairly and take proportionate action based on the circumstances.

## Enforcement

Depending on the situation, responses may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces.

## Maintaining this document

The project maintainer may update this policy as the community and project evolve.
